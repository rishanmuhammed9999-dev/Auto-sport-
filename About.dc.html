Who We Are

Auto Sports Car Care is a vehicle care workshop operated by AL KHAIR AL RAAZ LLC, licence number 2646563.01. We work on detailing, protection, paint and body, customisation, restoration and maintenance for cars brought to our workshop or collected from customers.

Every job starts with an inspection of the vehicle. Scope of work and materials are confirmed with the customer before a quotation is issued, and any additional work found during the job requires customer approval before it proceeds.
