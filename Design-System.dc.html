Icons & Borders

Line icons only, 1.6px stroke, no fill, sized 18–30px, coloured blue (#3f7bff) or muted (#9aa5ba) — never red. Borders are 1px, low-opacity blue (rgba(120,160,255,.12–.18)), brightening on hover/focus rather than adding weight.
